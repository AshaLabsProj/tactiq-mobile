## Full description

Skilltracker is a sideline and training-ground companion for youth football coaches. Record tactical events in two taps, keep an eye on match patterns, and assess player development across six core skills.

On match day, tap the pitch zone and record the action. Skilltracker gives you a calm, low-attention capture flow with optional pressure and player tags, haptic confirmation, undo, and a post-match tactical summary.

At practice, log a focused session, assess one player or the whole squad, set a development goal, and track whether practice focus is showing up in matches. Your data stays available offline and can be backed up to your signed-in Skilltracker account for use across devices.

Skilltracker is designed for coaches and authorised adults supporting youth football players.

## Reviewer notes

Skilltracker supports offline match capture. Core match-event recording does not require an account or network connection. A coach can create a local workspace, start a match, tap a pitch zone, choose an action, and review the summary without signing in. Sign-in is used only for optional multi-device cloud backup.

## Privacy disclosures

The release workflow should declare that the app may process coach account information and coach-entered youth player/team, practice, assessment, and match-event records in order to provide backup and cross-device restoration. The data is not sold, used for third-party advertising, or used for tracking. The account holder can erase their cloud mobile workspace in Settings; full account deletion is available by contacting the support address listed in the policy.
